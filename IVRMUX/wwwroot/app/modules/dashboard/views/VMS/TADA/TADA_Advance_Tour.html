Closure Leads:

Hot Leads:

Hot leads are those who have shown significant interest and engagement with our offerings. Identify leads who have recently interacted with our website, attended webinars, or engaged with our social media content. Arrange personalized meetings or demos during the tour to capitalize on their interest.

Cold Leads:

Cold leads are contacts who haven't shown much interest yet but have potential. Research companies or individuals who might benefit from our solutions but haven't engaged with us. Initiate contact through introductory emails or calls prior to the tour to warm them up.

Warm Leads:

Warm leads have shown some interest but may need nurturing before they are ready to make a decision. Engage with leads who have downloaded resources from our website or attended previous events. Offer them additional valuable content or insights to keep them engaged and move them closer to conversion.

Negotiation Leads:

These leads are already in negotiation stages but need further discussions or incentives to close the deal. Prioritize leads who have requested proposals or pricing information. Schedule meetings during the tour to address any concerns and finalize terms.

These leads are primarily for gathering market insights or expanding our database. Engage with industry contacts, competitors' clients, or attendees at relevant events.
