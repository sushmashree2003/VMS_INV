Campus Based AI Attendance System


   Campus Based AI Attendance System collates the information digitally which are already captured in our    IVRM Software:


   1. Face Recognition Technology

          In the Face Recognition technology, cameras capture images of individuals, and the system uses AI           algorithms to identify and verify each person based on their unique facial features.


          The recognition distance has been greatly extended up to 2 meters long, which significantly improves           maximum traffic rate. While normal cameras support 15-degree angle facial recognition, our AI           technology supports 30-degree angle facial recognition.


   2. Data Collection

          Once the individual is recognized through the Face Recognition Technology, the system records the           attendance data in real-time. This data can include the time of entry, exit, google location and other           relevant information.


   3. Machine Learning Algorithms

          The Campus Based AI Attendance System, enable the system continuously to learn and improve its           accuracy through machine learning algorithms. It can adapt to changes in appearance, such as           haircuts or wearing glasses, ensuring reliable identification, etc.


   4. Integration with Existing Systems

          Campus Based AI Attendance System can be easily integrated and configured with our VAPS Digital           Campus Attendance Module and HRMS Module which efficiently enables seamless and automated           processes.

          The total installation timeline will be 45 Days by assuming face recognition registration for total           number of students and Staff.
